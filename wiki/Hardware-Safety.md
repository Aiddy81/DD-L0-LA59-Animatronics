# Safety first

Read this before you touch the hardware. It only takes two minutes.

## For kids and teens

- **Under 16? Build with an adult.** Especially for soldering and anything with a battery.
- If something gets hot, smells of burning or makes smoke, **unplug it and step back**. Tell an adult.

## Soldering

- A soldering iron is hotter than an oven (about 350 °C). Always put it back in its stand.
- Solder in a room with fresh air, or with a fan blowing the smoke away from your face.
- Wear safety glasses: solder can spit.
- Wash your hands after soldering, and don't eat while you solder.

## Batteries and power

- Lola can run from a **LiPo battery**. LiPos are safe when treated well, but can catch fire if damaged.
  - Never bend, puncture or squash one.
  - Don't let the two wires touch each other.
  - Charge it only through the Feather's own charger, and don't leave it charging unattended.
  - Stop using a battery that's puffy or swollen.
- **Unplug USB and the battery before you change any wiring.**
- Check the battery plug's **red (+) and black (−)** wires match the board before you plug in. Plugs from other makers can be wired the other way round.

## Moving parts

- The wing servos are strong for their size and move suddenly. **Keep fingers out of the wing gaps** while the robot is powered, and don't hold a wing still while it's trying to move: it can pinch you and it wears out the servo.
- If a wing is stuck or buzzing, power off and find out why before trying again.

## The simulator is always safe

Everything in the simulator is pretend. Try anything you like there first: see [Try changes in the simulator](Try-Changes-in-the-Simulator).
